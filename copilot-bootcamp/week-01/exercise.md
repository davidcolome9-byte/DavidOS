# Week 1 Exercise: The Provisional Credit Briefing

**Time: 20 minutes. Work alone.**

Everything below is fabricated. Riverstone Credit Union does not exist. The policy numbers, timelines, ticket IDs, and people are invented for training. Do not substitute real material at any point.

**These timelines are fabricated and several of them differ from Regulation E on purpose.** Nothing in this exercise is a reference for a live claim. For real timing, provisional credit, and member notice requirements, use Riverstone's actual dispute procedure and ask Compliance. If you find yourself repeating a number from this exercise on a real call, stop.

## The scenario

You work in the digital contact center at Riverstone Credit Union. Over the last two weeks, agents have been getting the same call: a member reports an unauthorized debit card transaction and wants to know when the money comes back.

Agents are giving three different answers. Your supervisor asks you to produce a one page briefing that an agent can read in fifteen seconds while a member is on the line.

You have four internal documents. They do not fully agree with each other, and one of them is out of date. That is on purpose. Real policy stacks look like this.

## Your job

Produce a briefing that:

- Tells an agent what to say about timing for each claim type
- Is short enough to scan during a live call
- Flags anything the source documents do not actually answer
- Does not state anything the sources do not support

## Step by step

**Step 1.** Copy the entire synthetic source pack below into a Copilot chat message. Paste it as one block.

**Step 2.** Ask your first question in whatever way feels natural. Do not try to be clever. Save the response somewhere so you can compare later.

**Step 3.** Ask again, this time specifying format, length, scope, and what to do about missing information.

**Step 4.** Ask Copilot to find the conflicts and the gaps. Something like: "Where do these four documents disagree with each other, and what would a contact center agent need to know that none of them state?"

**Step 5.** Check every number in the output against the source text yourself. Not by asking Copilot. By reading. If a number in the output does not appear in the sources, note it.

**Step 6.** Write your final briefing. Under 200 words.

## Synthetic source pack

Copy from the line below through the end of DOC 4.

**DOC 1. Riverstone CU Card Dispute Handling Standard, version 4.2, effective March 2024**

Scope: unauthorized transactions on Riverstone consumer debit cards.

When a member reports an unauthorized debit card transaction, the agent opens a claim in the case system on the same call. The claim record carries two dates: the date the member first reported it and the date the record was keyed. Riverstone's investigation window runs from the report date.

Riverstone completes the investigation within 10 business days of claim open. If the investigation is not complete within 10 business days, Riverstone issues provisional credit to the member's account by the end of the 10th business day.

For claims involving a card that was reported lost or stolen before the transaction posted, the claim is routed to the Card Recovery queue in addition to the standard path. The investigation window and provisional credit handling are unchanged. Card Recovery pursues network recovery in parallel, and that process does not change what the member is told or when.

Members must be notified in writing of the outcome within 3 business days of the investigation closing.

---

**DOC 2. Contact Center Quick Reference Card, undated, printed copy at agent desks**

Debit card fraud claims:

- Open the claim while the member is on the phone
- Tell the member "up to 10 days for provisional credit"
- Escalate to the Fraud Investigations queue if the total claim is over 500 dollars
- If the member is traveling internationally, use the international claim path instead
- Provisional credit is not available for business accounts

---

**DOC 3. Email from Fraud Investigations, subject "Timeline change for new account claims," dated January 2025**

Team,

Effective immediately, claims involving a transaction that occurred within 30 days of the first deposit to the account follow the extended path. These claims get a 20 business day window in place of the standard 10, and provisional credit is issued at the end of that window if the investigation is still open. This is a longer window, not an exclusion from provisional credit.

This applies to consumer accounts only. Route these to Fraud Investigations on open.

Please update your desk references.

Thanks,
J. Alvarez, Fraud Investigations

---

**DOC 4. Riverstone CU Member Services FAQ, public website, last reviewed December 2023**

**How long does a debit card fraud claim take?**

Most claims are resolved within 10 business days. If we need more time, we will credit your account while we finish our review. You will receive a letter explaining the outcome once our review is complete.

**What if my card was lost or stolen?**

Report it immediately through the mobile app or by calling us. We will close the card and issue a replacement within 7 to 10 calendar days.

End of synthetic source pack.

## Things you will run into

The four documents conflict in at least three places. Do not resolve the conflicts by picking your favorite. The briefing should surface the conflict and say which source is most current, because that is the honest answer and it is what an agent actually needs.

There is at least one question a contact center agent gets constantly that none of these four documents answer. Find it.
