## Facilitator notes on these posts

Naming the midpoint helps. People who have attended twice are deciding right now whether to attend four more times, and "this is the one that makes the rest work" gives them a reason.

The line about opening with your own bad prompt is doing real recruiting work. It signals that the room is safe for people who feel behind, and those are exactly the people whose attendance is at risk in Week 3.

Do not soften the paired work. Some people will dislike it. Announcing it clearly means they show up prepared rather than surprised, and surprise is what makes people leave the call.

If someone asks to skip the pairing, let them work solo and swap prompts with you instead. Do not make it a fight. You lose more by turning it into a confrontation than you gain from the exercise.
