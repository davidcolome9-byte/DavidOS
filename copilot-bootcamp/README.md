# Microsoft 365 Copilot Boot Camp

Six weekly hours that take a contact center team from asking Copilot for things to checking what it gives back, writing with it under real stakes, and packaging the result so a colleague can use it without learning any of this.

## Start here

**Running it for the first time?** Read `shared/syllabus.md`, then Week 1's facilitator guide. Everything else can wait until the week you need it.

**Handed a week to run?** Open that week's folder. The six files are always in the same order and always do the same jobs.

**Just want the prompts?** `shared/prompt-library.md`. Fifty of them, sorted by the work rather than by the tool.

## How a session runs

An hour: a short teach, a live demo, twenty minutes where people do the work themselves, and a debrief. Nobody watches a tool tour. Each week ends with a submission that takes fifteen to forty five minutes, and the honest cost is printed on every participant one pager rather than buried.

Week 6 is different. Everyone presents one improved workflow with a before and after number. A five minute slot costs six and a half once you add the question and the screen handoff, so plan the cohort against that before promising anyone a slot.

## Ground rules

- Every exercise uses fabricated material for a fictional institution. No real member names, account or card numbers, case identifiers, or screenshots of live systems, in any session.
- The synthetic policies differ from real regulation on purpose. No timeline, provisional credit rule, or member notice requirement leaves these exercises and enters live work.
- Five prompts in the library are marked for live case work. Those are de-identified before use or they are not used.
- Every word that leaves the building has a human owner. Not the tool.

## Adapting it

The material assumes a cohort of 12 to 16, submissions collected in a Teams thread, and mixed tool access, so no exercise depends on Copilot in Excel or on permission to create agents. Change any of that and the sessions still run. `shared/MANIFEST.md` lists the calls made where the brief was open.

Riverstone Credit Union, its policies, metrics, claims, and people are invented for training and do not exist.
