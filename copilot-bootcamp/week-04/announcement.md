## Facilitator notes on these posts

The two sentence comparison in the post is the whole session in miniature. Lead with it. It gets people who think they do not need a writing session to show up, because the point is clearly not about writing well.

Announce the read aloud in advance. People who hate reading aloud will otherwise leave when you spring it on them. Announcing it also means they write with a listener in mind, which improves the work before the session even starts.

Say "I will go first" and then actually go first. If you skip it, nobody else will do it.

Do not use the words "compliance training" anywhere in the announcement, even though part of what you are teaching is a control. The moment it sounds like compliance training, attendance drops and the people who do come arrive defensive.
