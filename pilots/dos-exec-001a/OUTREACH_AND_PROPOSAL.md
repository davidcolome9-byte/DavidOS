## Proposal outline

### 1. Context

The fictional bakehouse has appealing products but an unclear digital
decision path. The opportunity is to make signature items, timing, and
use cases easier to understand before selecting commerce tooling.

### 2. Objective

Create an approved, fact-checked local website starting point that helps
a first-time visitor understand what to choose and how pickup works.

### 3. Proposed scope

- discovery and fact-verification session;
- opportunity and message brief;
- signature-product hierarchy;
- responsive static website adaptation;
- local preorder-path prototype;
- mobile, keyboard, link, and basic accessibility validation;
- local handoff package with publishing/integration options left as
  separate decisions.

### 4. Deliverables

1. Approved messaging brief.
2. Responsive local website source.
3. Up to six verified product entries.
4. One local-only preorder interaction.
5. Accessibility and responsiveness report.
6. Content/asset checklist.
7. Final approval bundle.

### 5. Timeline assumption

Synthetic planning assumption: two weeks from receipt of verified
content and one consolidated decision maker. This is not a real
commitment.

### 6. Commercial assumption

Synthetic planning assumption: fixed fee of **$3,800**, 50% at kickoff
and 50% at approved local handoff. No payment is requested or
authorized.

### 7. Client responsibilities

- provide and approve accurate menu, price, lead-time, schedule,
  allergen, contact, and policy details;
- supply owned or properly licensed imagery;
- identify one final approver;
- obtain legal, regulatory, trademark, accessibility, and food-labeling
  advice where applicable;
- approve any later hosting, commerce, analytics, messaging, or provider
  integration separately.

### 8. Exclusions

Ordering, payment, inventory, accounts, CRM, analytics, email/SMS,
delivery, photography production, legal advice, domain purchase,
hosting, publication, and ongoing maintenance.

### 9. Acceptance

- approved facts replace every fictional value;
- primary tasks work at agreed mobile and desktop sizes;
- no broken links or missing assets;
- keyboard path and basic semantics pass;
- approval is recorded before any publication or connection;
- no scope-excluded capability is implied.

### 10. Change control

Any live form, payment, inventory, provider, analytics, hosting,
publication, messaging, or off-device data flow becomes a separately
estimated and explicitly authorized package.
